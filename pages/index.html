Protocol Emulator

An entry for Jane Street's protocol emulator ASIC competition, written in Hardcaml and built on IHP CMOS5L through Tiny Tapeout, in 6 x 4 tiles at 48 MHz.

      The hardened die, cells and wires lit as they switch.
