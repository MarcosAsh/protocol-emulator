Protocol Emulator

An entry for Jane Street's protocol emulator ASIC competition, written in Hardcaml and built on IHP CMOS5L through Tiny Tapeout, in 6 x 4 tiles at 50 MHz.

    The hardened die, cells and wires lit as they switch.

  • Firmware timing playground

    The assembler, the timing analyser and the kernel, compiled to JavaScript with js_of_ocaml.
