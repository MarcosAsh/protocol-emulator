Refusal
If the kernel or analyser refuses the firmware because the spec's timing cannot be met
(a deadline wait that may be missed, a slack that is negative), do not weaken the kernel or
loosen the spec. Try the honest alternatives (a different firmware shape, a slower period the
spec allows). If none works, stop and name the limit: its parameter, the spec's number and
page, and the kernel's message.
